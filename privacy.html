Privacy Policy for Word Calculator

Last updated: October 2026

Summary

Word Calculator does not collect, store on any server, share or sell your personal information.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your calculation history, the calculation you were working on, and your settings (language, theme, button sound) only on your own device, using the browser storage built into the app. This data never leaves your device. You can erase the history from the app (Settings > View history > Clear history), or remove all data by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage.

What the app does not do

  • It does not require an account or login.
  • It does not ask for any device permission (such as contacts, location, camera, microphone or storage).
  • It does not show advertisements.
  • It does not use analytics or tracking tools.
  • It does not send any information to third parties.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
